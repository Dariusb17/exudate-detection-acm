    How Exora finds exudates

    It implements the exudate part (Section III-A) of Rostamian & Shekari's adaptive color morphology method. Four stages, in this order:

    1. Pre-processing

      Colors are converted to CIE L*a*b* and matched to a reference photo, so every camera looks alike.

    2. Candidate extraction

      Adaptive color morphology highlights regions close to the exudate color, measured with CIEDE2000.

    3. Look-alike removal

      The optic disc, blood vessels and the border of the image are taken out of the candidates.

    4. Segmentation & refinement

      Morphological reconstruction, a brightness threshold and a Sobel edge filter keep the real lesions.

    Method

    M. Rostamian, M. Shekari. Designing an Adaptive Color Morphology Operator for Exudates and Microaneurysms Detection in Color Fundus Images. IEEE Access, vol. 14, pp. 54937–54953, 2026.
